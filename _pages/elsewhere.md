Not everything I am interested in has to do with economics.

I read more fiction than anything else. I tend to gravitate toward science fiction, mystery, psychological fiction, and stories that spend a lot of time inside a character's head. I like books that leave something to think about after the last page. Classic English literature has a permanent place on my reading list, especially Austen, Brontë, and Eliot. I also return to classic Persian literature, which feels like a different kind of conversation with the past.

I have a similar weakness for stories built around mysteries, puzzles, and questions that take time to unravel. On screen, some of the shows I've enjoyed most include *Severance*, *Silo*, *Stranger Things*, and *Sherlock*. Apparently, I have a fairly consistent preference for stories where something is not quite what it first appears to be.

I'm slowly learning French. Progress is not particularly fast, but I like the process of learning a language and noticing how much a different language changes the way an idea is expressed.

I also like cooking, especially when it gives me an excuse to experiment rather than follow a recipe exactly. My results are inconsistent, but the process is part of the fun. I have a particular weakness for pasta, fries, chicken, chocolate, and Istanbul-style food.

More generally, I like having a warm, comfortable home to come back to. I enjoy quiet routines, reading, watching something good at the end of the day, and occasionally disappearing into a completely different subject for no practical reason.

This is the part of the website least concerned with what I am supposed to be doing.
